The Group

We are an independent group operating through two specialized, segregated entities: Trading and Global.

Three Reefs Group is a privately held firm incorporated in the Cayman Islands with headquarters at Signal House, George Town. We are a registered member of the Cayman Commodities and Derivatives Centre. The Group is wholly owned by its founder and has no outside investors.

The Group operates through two distinct entities. Three Reefs Trading acts as a single-family office and invests its proprietary capital. Three Reefs Global delivers services to outside partners and builds the technology that powers the entire Group. The two entities are kept strictly separate to isolate the capital at risk from the operating business.

Activities

We trade our own capital with high conviction and provide bespoke services to institutional clients.

 

The Group executes an unconstrained and opportunistic trading strategy focused on analytical rigor, structural inefficiencies, special situations, and asymmetric opportunities. Our portfolio is generally concentrated in a few high-conviction positions and proprietary strategies that are fundamentally unrelated to one another. We invest exclusively in liquid, exchange-traded commodities, securities, and derivatives across global markets. We do not solicit or accept external capital.


We also provide specialized solutions and services to qualified institutional financial entities and professional clients. Engagements typically involve complex mandates across multiple jurisdictions and asset classes, requiring a high degree of technical expertise and professional discretion. We do not engage with, or offer our services to, retail or individual customers.

Operations

Our research, investment process, and systems for portfolio and treasury management are all developed in-house.

 

We manage our execution and treasury infrastructure independently, with banking and payment capabilities in more than 20 countries and 30 currencies, and access to over 150 markets worldwide.


Our systems are built front to back on a cloud-native architecture, providing a consolidated view of every position and account across the Group. Everything rests on our own capabilities: we do not rely on third-party research or data vendors, and we do not allocate capital to outside managers.


We are always looking for excellent operational partners. If you are a bank, broker, or execution venue offering competitive pricing, our door is open and we would be delighted to hear from you.
